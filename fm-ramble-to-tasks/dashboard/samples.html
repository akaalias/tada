A person talks — often rambling, sometimes correcting themselves mid-sentence — and the app has to turn that into a clean list of tasks: zero, one, or many. When nothing in the ramble is actionable, an empty list is the right answer.

A frontier cloud model (Claude Sonnet) already does this well. The goal of this research is to do it just as well with the small Foundation Model that ships on-device — no network, no cloud. The samples below are the target: each pairs a real-style ramble with the gold task list Sonnet produces. The evolutionary run keeps tuning the on-device model until its output matches these golds on our fitness metric.

So this page is the problem statement made concrete — the bar we're trying to clear, case by case, grouped by what each one stresses.
